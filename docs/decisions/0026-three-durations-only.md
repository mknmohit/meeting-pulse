# Manual override uses seven presets plus a Station custom duration

**Status:** PROPOSED
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Supersedes:** -

## Context

The owner needs quick choices during a meeting rather than an open-ended scheduler.

## Decision

Keep 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, and Until I
clear it as the seven fixed choices. The Station also offers a Custom choice
from 1 minute through 24 hours. The menu bar keeps the seven fixed choices so
it remains scannable during a meeting.

## Consequences

The menu remains scannable, while the Station can support precise hours and
minutes without a new persistence format.
