# Manual override uses seven fixed durations

**Status:** PROPOSED
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Supersedes:** -

## Context

The owner needs quick choices during a meeting rather than an open-ended scheduler.

## Decision

Offer 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 45 minutes, 1 hour, 2 hours, 4 hours, and Until I clear it.

## Consequences

The menu remains scannable and the duration list stays shared across the app.
