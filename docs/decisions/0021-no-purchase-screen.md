# No purchase screens

**Status:** PROPOSED
**Date:** 2026-08-08
**Supersedes:** -

## Context

The MVP is a single-owner utility: one person runs the macOS app and one
viewer reads the page. There is no billing, no upgrade flow, and no
account-level entitlement in scope.

## Decision

Do not add purchase, upgrade, or subscription screens to the owner app.

## Consequences

The Station stays task-focused and no monetization copy can distract from the signal.

## Revisit later

If a second owner joins or multi-tenant hosting is introduced, this decision
should be revisited. The underlying reason is "no one in the product ever
needs to pay for anything today", not "this product must never have
payments".
