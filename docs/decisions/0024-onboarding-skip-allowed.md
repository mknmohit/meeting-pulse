# Accessibility onboarding can be skipped

**Status:** PROPOSED
**Date:** 2026-08-08

## Context

An owner may want to inspect the sharing flow before granting Accessibility.

## Decision

Keep `Skip for now` on onboarding Step 2 and continue to Step 3.

## Consequences
